Do you have an electrical emergency?

If you are experiencing any of the following, call us immediately. Do not attempt to fix these issues yourself.

Power Outage

Sudden, unexplained loss of power to part or all of your property while neighbors have power.

Electrical Shocks

Receiving a mild shock when touching an appliance or switch.

Burning Smell

A fishy or burning plastic smell coming from an outlet or the breaker panel.

Breaker Won't Reset

A circuit breaker that continually trips and will not stay in the ON position.

Water Damage

Water leaking into light fixtures, outlets, or the main electrical panel.

Sparks & Arcing

Visible sparks, popping sounds, or humming noises from electrical components.
