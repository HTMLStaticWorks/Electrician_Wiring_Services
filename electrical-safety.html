Electrical Safety

Protecting your property from hazards with comprehensive audits and code compliance.

Critical Hazards to Watch For

If you notice any of these signs, contact an electrician immediately to prevent fires or shocks.

Sparking Outlets

If plugging in a device causes a spark or pop, the outlet is severely compromised.

Burning Smells

An acrid smell near switches or panels indicates wires are overheating and melting.

Tripping Breakers

Frequent trips mean your circuit is overloaded or there is a dangerous short circuit.

Flickering Lights

Often a sign of loose connections which can arc and cause electrical fires.

Our Comprehensive Safety Audit

We conduct a meticulous 40-point inspection to ensure your property meets and exceeds the National Electrical Code (NEC).

  • Verify proper grounding of all systems.
  • Test GFCI and AFCI outlets for correct tripping.
  • Thermal imaging of breaker panels to detect heat.
  • Inspect wire insulation for degradation or pests.
  • Ensure smoke/CO detectors are hardwired properly.
  • Calculate overall load capacity against main service.

Live Thermal Scan Demo

Thermal imaging reveals hidden electrical faults by detecting abnormal heat signatures. A red hotspot indicates severe overheating that could cause an electrical fire or equipment failure.
